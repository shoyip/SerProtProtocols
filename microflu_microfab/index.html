Microfabrication of PDMS chips

This protocol takes two to three days.

Molding of chips

Time: 1h30 / 2h

Materials

  • Momentive RTV615 (PDMS base "A" and curing agent "B")
  • Disposable tray
  • Spatula
  • Weight scale
  • Four Falcon 50mL tubes
  • Centrifuge
  • Vacuum machine
  • Epoxy molds for the chips in Petri dishes
  • Aluminium foil

Procedure

  1. Pour liquids "A" (135g) and "B" (15g) in a disposable tray on the weight scale and mix with the aid of a spatula for 1min. Pour the mix equally in four Falcon 50mL tubes.
  2. Centrifuge the Falcon 50mL tubes at 4000rpm for 15min.
  3. Lay the molds on two perpendicular aluminium foils as wide as the molds as to form a cross. Fold the sheets on the edges to form a rim of half a centimeter.
  4. Pour the content of each Falcon tube in each mold.
  5. Put the molds in their Petri dishes and leave them in the vacuum chamber for approx. 1h.
  6. Bake them in the oven overnight.

Warning

If the room is too warm, the PDMS might polymerize during the centrifuging or the vacuum.

Tip

If there are bubbles before baking, use P10 tips in order to pop them.

Cutting, hole punching and plasma bonding

Time: 1h30 / 2h

Materials

  • PDMS chips
  • Cutter
  • Hole punching pens (0.75mm green pen and 0.50mm blue pen)
  • Cutting mat
  • 50mm x 75mm pre-cleaned Millipore microscope glass slide
  • Isopropanol, 70% ethanol, MilliQ water

Procedure

  1. Take the molds out of the oven. Remove the aluminium foil. Cut the chips out of the mold and gently lift the edges. If the chip is sticking to the mold, use isopropanol to ease the removal.

Warning

If using wafer chips, be extremely careful at this step. Wafer chips are extremely delicate and easily break. Avoid putting shear stress on the PDMS while tearing the aluminium foil and while removing the PDMS.

  1. Under the hood use the green (0.75mm, electrode holes) and blue (0.50mm, fluid holes) pens in order to punch holes. On the encapsulation chip there are only fluid holes. On the sorting chip the central row of holes are electrode holes, and the rest is for fluids. Holes should be punched vertically on a cutting mat.
  2. Wash glass slides and the marked side of the chips using in the order isopropanol, ethanol and water, then dry the surface using compressed air. Deposit at most two glass slides in the plasma machine, then lay the PDMS chips on top with the marked side on top. Close the door of the plasma machine and turn the arrow shaped valve to the left.
  3. Turn on the plasma machine by setting the switch to ON. Lift the red lever of the oxygen tank in order to open it. Turn the vacuum machine on and wait until the pressure drops below 1mbar. Then open the round valve on the plasma machine in order to let oxygen into the chamber for a couple of seconds. Wait until the pressure drops below 1mbar. Repeat 3/4 times.

Warning

It is crucial to turn the plasma machine a bit before you actually use it, otherwise you might get uncomplete or unsuccessful bondings.

Tip

If the vacuum machine is too slow, try wiping the seal of the chamber with a damp cloth.

  1. Turn the dial on the plasma machine from OFF to HIGH. Turn off the lights in the room: you should see a purple / white glow in the chamber. Wait 45sec before turning the dial back to OFF and turn the switch to OFF as well. Put the red lever of the oxygen tank down. Turn the vacuum machine off. Turn the arrow shaped valve to the right in order to let the air in.

Warning

A plasma cycle should not last more than 1min, otherwise the surface activation might not work at all. Keep it between 30sec and 1min.

  1. Take the glass slides and chips out of the oven and, once at a time, flip both of them and let the marked side of the chip stick to the glass slide by gently pushing. Pushing too hard will result in blocked channels.

Silane waterproofing and soldering

Materials

  • PDMS chips
  • Tweezers
  • Sigmacote siliconizing reagent
  • 1mL syringe
  • Tubing
  • Indium solder wire
  • Hot plate
  • Voltmeter
  • Scotch tape

Procedure

  1. Under the hood, insert the tubing in the needle with the aid of a tweezer. Draw a small amount of siliconizing reagent using the syringe, then insert the other side of the tubing in the fluid holes and fill them with the liquid until they come out from another hole. At this point, aspirate back the liquid. Repeat for every hole of the chip.
  2. Turn on the hot plate at 75°C. Leave the chips for 5-10 minutes on top of the plate. Then insert the solder wire into the electrode hole with the aid of a tweezer: as soon as it will reach the glass slide it will start to melt. Check that the melted metal arrived at the second electrode, and repeat for each other electrode hole. At the end, install the electrodes by putting them in the holes.

Warning

The electrodes should be installed as soon as the soldering is done. Installing electrodes when the soldered parts are cold might lead to a non-functional electrical circuit.

  1. Check that the electrodes are working correctly. After a visual inspection, you can use the voltmeter by turning its dial to the diode symbol. Two by two, touch one electrode with one probe of the voltmeter and the other with another probe. The voltemeter beeps if the soldered circuit is connected.
  2. Tape the circuits that have been tested and are ready to use.

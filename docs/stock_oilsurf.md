# Make a stock of oil with 10% surfactant

[Original protocol](https://www.emulseo.com/wp-content/uploads/FluoSurf%E2%84%A2-dilution-protocol.pdf) from Emusleo.

## Materials

- 3M Novec 7500 Engineered Fluid (aka mineral oil)
- Amber vial
- [Emulseo FluoSurf-C](https://www.emulseo.com/products/stabilizer/) surfactant
- Roller shaker

## Procedure

1. Weigh 1.6948g of surfactant.
2. In an amber vial, weigh 16.1g of mineral oil.
3. Put the surfactant inside the amber vial.
4. Stir on a roller shaker at 30rpm overnight at room
   temperature.

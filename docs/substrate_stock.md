# Make substrate stock

## Materials

- DMSO
- Lyophilized peptide substrate
- PCR tubes
- Falcon 50mL

## Procedure

1. Unseal the aluminium cap of the vial containing the
   lyophilized stock of peptide substrate. Open the rubber
   cap.
2. Our final goal is to have the liquid substrate stock at
   **10 mM**. The volume of DMSO we need to mix in the vial
   is V = m / (mw * M). For example, if m = 5mg, mw = 
   1053.13g/mol and we want to attain a stock concentration
   of M = 10mM, we will need to add 475uL of DMSO in
   the vial.
3. Make aliquots of 10uL or 20uL each in PCR tubes and stock
   them in a Falcon tube.
4. Store the Falcon tube at -20°C.

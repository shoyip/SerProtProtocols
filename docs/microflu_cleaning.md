# Cleaning of the Fluigent Flow Units

[Original protocol](https://www.fluigent.com/resources-support/support-tools/downloads/user-manuals/cleaning-procedure-flow-units/) from Fluigent.

## Materials

- Fluigent Flow Unit S/M
- Fluigent tube
- Isopropanol, MilliQ water
- 3M Novec Engineered Fluid (aka mineral oil)
- Tergazyme

## Procedure

Before each rig run where the Flow Units are used:

1. Let mineral oil through the critical Flow Units for 5min.

After each rig run where the Flow Units are used:

1. Let isopropanol run at 3.5uL/min (S) or 49uL/min (M)
   through the Flow Units until two or three drops are
   formed at the other end of the Flow Units.
2. Use an empty tube at maximum pressure or compressed air
   to dry the Flow Unit.

A couple of times a month, do a thorough cleaning using
Tergazyme. The recommended flows are always 3.5uL/min (S) or
49uL/min (M).

1. Rinse Flow Units using mineral oil for 5min.
2. Make Tergazyme solution: use 10g/L of dry Tergazyme in
   warm water (55°C).
3. Fill the tubings and the Flow Units with Tergazyme. Now
   stop the flow. Let the Tergazyme stay in the system for
   two hours.
4. Rinse with MilliQ water for 30min.
5. Rinse with isopropanol for 30min.
6. Let air at maximum pressure for 10min.

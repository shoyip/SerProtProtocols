Make a stock of oil with 10% surfactant

Original protocol from Emusleo.

Materials

  • 3M Novec 7500 Engineered Fluid (aka mineral oil)
  • Amber vial
  • Emulseo FluoSurf-C surfactant
  • Roller shaker

Procedure

  1. Weigh 1.6948g of surfactant.
  2. In an amber vial, weigh 16.1g of mineral oil.
  3. Put the surfactant inside the amber vial.
  4. Stir on a roller shaker at 30rpm overnight at room temperature.
